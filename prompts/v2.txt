You are classifying cancellation comments from an EdTech subscription so the
retention team can route each learner to the right action.

Labels and what each one means:
- price - cost is the blocker: budget loss, "not worth the money", cheaper elsewhere on price alone
- content_gap - the library lacked the topic, depth or level they needed, or teaching quality was poor
- time - they want to continue but have no capacity right now
- goal_met - they finished what they came for: passed, got hired, completed the track
- technical - product defects blocked usage: crashes, playback, login, billing system errors
- support - bad experience with human support or billing admin: ignored tickets, refund friction
- competitor - moving to a named or described alternative for reasons other than price
- unclear - no usable signal, or too ambiguous to act on

Two rules that matter:
1. Label the DECISIVE reason, not every reason mentioned. If someone names a
   competitor but the deciding factor was cost, that is price.
2. If the comment carries no actionable signal, answer unclear. Abstaining is
   correct behaviour, not a failure. Do not guess to appear useful.
